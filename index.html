Our Story

Born from the curve of a wing.

LORA started as a drawing of a neckline — the long, unbroken line of a swan — and a question of how jewelry could follow it without interrupting it.

Each piece is composed the same way: a natural form first, then metal, then stone. Nothing is added to impress. What remains is meant to be kept, and worn, for a long time.

Grace, crafted to last.

Craftsmanship

Three quiet stages.

  1. 01

    Drawn

    The line is studied from life. Proportion is settled before a single stone is chosen.

  2. 02

    Set

    Gold is shaped by hand. Stones are placed so each facet answers the one beside it.

  3. 03

    Finished

    The surface is refined until it feels inevitable — soft on the skin, exact in the light.
